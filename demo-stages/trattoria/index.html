Our Story

A neighborhood table, set by hand since 1998.

Nonna Vita opened Bella Vista with eight tables and one rule: nothing leaves the kitchen she wouldn't serve her own family. Three decades on, her grandson Marco still rolls the tagliatelle before the first espresso, and the salt-baked branzino still arrives whole, cracked open at the table.

The menu follows the dock and the garden, not the calendar — what's best this week is what you'll eat. The cellar runs deep on coastal Italian whites; the patio runs late on Friday and Saturday.
