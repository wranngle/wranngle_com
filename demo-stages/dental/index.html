Questions, answered

Probably what you wanted to ask.

Don't see your question? Tap the assistant in the corner — it can book, reschedule, or get a real human on the phone.

Do you take my insurance?

Most likely, yes. We're in-network with the major carriers (Delta, MetLife, Cigna, Aetna, Guardian, BCBS, United Concordia) and we file for out-of-network plans too. Send a photo of your card when you book and we'll text you what's covered before you arrive.

What happens at a same-day emergency?

Call (555) 555‑0199 or use the assistant in the corner. We hold slots every day for emergencies. After a brief phone triage we'll either see you within hours or, if it's safer, route you to the right kind of care.

Are you good with kids?

Our hygiene team is trained for pediatric visits — kid-sized chairs, a sticker wall, and a "show, tell, do" approach. First visits are typically short and end with a tooth-fairy starter pack.

How much will whitening actually move my teeth?

Our in-office treatment guarantees a 2-shade lift on the VITA scale on the first visit, with take-home trays included to push another shade or two over the following week.

Do you offer payment plans?

Yes. CareCredit and our in-house 0% plans cover larger treatments (veneers, implants, ortho). We'll show you the monthly number alongside the treatment plan — no pressure.

Book your visit.

Pick a time, snap a photo of your insurance card, and we'll handle the rest. Or call us — there's almost always a human on the line.
